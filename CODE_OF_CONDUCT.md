# Code of conduct

We want project spaces to be calm, constructive, and welcoming. Treat other participants with respect; critique ideas and evidence rather than people; avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and disclosure of private information. Assume good intent while accepting correction, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior undermines those expectations. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical.
